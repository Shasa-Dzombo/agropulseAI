## 📊 DETAILED CROP ANALYSIS

### 1. Sweet Potato (Ipomoea batatas) - 765 lines

**Market Context:** $28B global, 90M tons/year, 6-12 month storage common

**Critical Diseases (8):**
- **Black Rot** (Ceratocystis fimbriata): #1 STORAGE DISEASE GLOBALLY
  - $500M+ annual losses worldwide
  - 30-60% storage loss without control
  - Sweet fruity odor DIAGNOSTIC
  - Bitter taste develops = total rejection
  - Circular black lesions start at wounds
  
- **Bacterial Soft Rot** (Erwinia): RAPID STORAGE DESTROYER
  - Complete bin loss in 7-14 days if not controlled
  - Foul odor, mushy tissue
  - Temperature control CRITICAL: 13-16°C
  - >18°C = bacterial epidemic
  
- **Fusarium Wilt**: Soilborne lethal, races 0-6, 5+ year field abandonment
- **Scurf** (Monilochaetes): Cosmetic only, fresh market rejection
- **Feathery Mottle Virus** (SPFMV): 20-60% yield loss, aphid-borne
- **Soil Rot** (Streptomyces): Pore rot, pH <5.2 favors

**Critical Technology - CURING PROTOCOL:**
```
Temperature: 29-32°C (85-90°F)
Humidity: 85-90% RH
Duration: 4-7 days
Effect: Heals wounds, reduces rot 70-90%
```

**Storage Requirements:**
- Temperature: 13-16°C (55-60°F)
- WARNING: >18°C = bacterial soft rot epidemic
- WARNING: <13°C = chilling injury + secondary infections
- Humidity: 85-90% RH
- Duration: Up to 12 months with proper curing

### 2. Carrot (Daucus carota) - 590 lines

**Market Context:** $10B global, 44M tons/year, baby carrot 40% premium

**Critical Diseases (8):**
- **Alternaria Leaf Blight** (Alternaria dauci): #1 DISEASE WORLDWIDE
  - 50-80% yield loss potential
  - $200M+ annual damage globally
  - Can destroy crop in 2-3 weeks
  - Target spot pattern with yellow halos DIAGNOSTIC
  - FRAC rotation critical: 11 → 7 → 3 → M5
  - Spray intervals: 7-14 days based on weather
  
- **Cavity Spot** (Pythium violae, P. sulcatum): MARKET REJECTION
  - Elliptical sunken pits on root surface
  - Cosmetic disaster, fresh market total rejection
  - Processing market accepts
  - No effective fungicides
  - DRAINAGE ONLY CONTROL
  
- **Aster Yellows** (Phytoplasma): BITTER ROOTS
  - Leafhopper vector
  - Excessive branching (witches' broom) diagnostic
  - Bitter taste = unmarketable
  - No cure, vector control only
  
- **Cercospora**: Small circular spots with gray centers
- **Bacterial Soft Rot**: Storage destroyer, 0-2°C critical
- **Powdery Mildew**: Late-season, dry weather disease

**FRAC Fungicide Rotation (Alternaria):**
```
Week 1: FRAC M5 (Chlorothalonil) - protectant
Week 2: FRAC 11+3 (Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole)
Week 3: FRAC 7 (Boscalid)
Week 4: Rotate back to M5
Continue 7-14 day intervals until harvest
Cost: $250/acre/season
```

**Storage Protocol:**
- Temperature: 0-2°C (32-36°F)
- Humidity: 95-100% RH
- Rapid cooling after harvest essential
- Remove wounded roots before storage

### 3. Broccoli (Brassica oleracea var. italica) - 671 lines

**Market Context:** $15B global, 26M tons/year, $1.50-3.00/lb fresh

**Critical Diseases (8):**
- **Black Rot** (Xanthomonas campestris): QUARANTINE WORLDWIDE
  - V-shaped yellow lesions DIAGNOSTIC
  - Black veins PATHOGNOMONIC (vascular infection)
  - Hot water seed treatment: 50°C × 25 minutes MANDATORY
  - NO EFFECTIVE CHEMICAL CONTROL
  - Regulatory notification required
  - Export restrictions apply
  
- **Clubroot** (Plasmodiophora brassicae): 20+ YEAR SOIL SURVIVAL
  - Most persistent horticultural pathogen
  - Swollen root clubs diagnostic
  - Resting spores survive 20+ years
  - pH >7.2 critical for suppression
  - Resistance genes: Crr1, Crr2, Crr3, Crr4, Rcr1
  - 19+ pathotypes identified (resistance breakdown)
  - Field abandonment 7+ years
  - $1B+ global losses
  
- **Downy Mildew** (Peronospora parasitica): HEAD ROT CATASTROPHIC
  - Purple-gray sporulation on heads = 100% market rejection
  - 48-72 hour progression to total loss
  - Cannot be removed by washing
  - 7-day spray intervals CRITICAL near heading
  - Cost: $300/acre/season
  
- **Head Rot Complex** (Pseudomonas, Erwinia): 48-HOUR EMERGENCY
  - Water-soaked florets progress to slime
  - Complete head disintegration in 48-72 hours
  - NO SALVAGE once symptoms appear
  - Avoid overhead irrigation at heading
  
- **Alternaria Leaf Spot**: Target spots, defoliation
- **White Rust** (Albugo candida): Quarantine some regions
- **Blackleg** (Phoma lingam): Stem girdling

**Clubroot Management Strategy:**
```
Soil pH: Lime to >7.2 (critical)
Varieties: Plant resistant only (Crr genes)
Rotation: 7+ years from brassicas (20+ for full suppression)
Equipment: Clean thoroughly (spores spread on soil)
Weeds: Control cruciferous weeds (hosts)
Field Selection: Avoid clubroot history areas
Resting Spores: 20+ year survival documented
```

**Head Quality Protocol:**
- Downy mildew on heads = 100% rejection
- Head rot = 48-hour window to harvest
- Prevention only strategy (no cure)
- Daily scouting during heading critical

## 🔬 DIAGNOSTIC SYMPTOM ALGORITHMS

### Shape-Based Detection:
1. **V-shaped lesions**: Black rot (broccoli) - margin to midrib
2. **Circular lesions**: Black rot (sweet potato), Cercospora (carrot)
3. **Elliptical pits**: Cavity spot (carrot) - elongated at lenticels
4. **Angular spots**: Downy mildew (broccoli) - follow leaf veins
5. **Target spots**: Alternaria - concentric rings

### Color-Based Detection:
1. **Black veins**: Vascular infection (broccoli black rot)
2. **Purple-gray sporulation**: Downy mildew (broccoli)
3. **Yellow halos**: Alternaria (carrot)
4. **Gray centers**: Cercospora (carrot) vs brown Alternaria
5. **White powder**: Powdery mildew

### Odor/Texture Diagnostics:
1. **Sweet fruity odor**: Black rot (sweet potato) - pathognomonic
2. **Foul bacterial odor**: Soft rot (all crops)
3. **Bitter taste**: Black rot (sweet potato), Aster yellows (carrot)
4. **Water-soaked**: Head rot (broccoli), soft rot
5. **Mushy tissue**: Bacterial diseases complete disintegration
